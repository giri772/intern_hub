ABOUT INTERNHUB

Helping students turn potential into opportunity.

InternHub is a student-focused internship discovery platform designed to make exploring career opportunities easier. We bring opportunities from different domains into one simple, accessible and responsive interface.

Whether you are interested in software development, artificial intelligence, electronics, data science, cybersecurity or design, InternHub helps you explore your interests and take the next step towards professional growth.
